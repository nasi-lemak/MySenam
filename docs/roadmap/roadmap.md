# Roadmap

Ideas not yet built, and a record of what has been delivered from them.
**Nothing in the ideas table is operational.** What works is in
[feature status](../product/feature-status.md); what is broken or unverified
is in [known limitations](known-limitations.md), and fixing those comes before
anything here.

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| PLANNED | Intended: what it needs is known, but it is not scheduled |
| PROPOSED | Raised by research, an audit or a conversation; no decision to build it |
| BLOCKED | Cannot start without a partner, credential, approval or decision |
| DEFERRED | Postponed on purpose, with the reason given |

A row moves to [delivered](#delivered) when its acceptance criterion is
checked, not when code exists. Research recommendations and audit findings
are added here as PROPOSED until the owner decides.

| Idea | Status | Why, and what it needs |
| ---- | ------ | ---------------------- |
| Per-person credentials through a smart-lock vendor (TTLock, Igloohome; Igloohome's offline algoPIN suits a door with no wifi), so an unstaffed resource issues one credential per person instead of one code per lock | BLOCKED | Vendor credentials. The induction record and the customer's entry screen are already the right shape for it. It is what 24-hour gym chains use, and the biggest gap in unstaffed access. |
| Further work on supporting documents and their infrastructure: PDF processing or sanitisation, other scanning engines, the scanner's memory, document files out of Postgres (Spaces, S3 and GuardDuty, B2, R2), upload latency, previews or conversion, measuring the scanner on a real Droplet, and the off-site erasure ledger against a real bucket | DEFERRED | The owner's decision of 27 October 2026: a temporary freeze while the remaining functional limitations are completed, not a decision against PDFs or scanning. What is built stays as it is (quarantine, scanning before release, failing closed, retention); the open verification items stay in [known limitations](known-limitations.md#integrations-awaiting-live-verification-or-a-partner) and the options in the [research note](../research/2026-10-27-document-storage-and-scanning-costs.md). |
| Sign-up by an emailed link, the form always answering "check your email" | PROPOSED | Raised by the account-enumeration work (27 October 2026): sign-up is the one place left that says an address already has an account (rate-limited per client, [known limitations](known-limitations.md#operations-and-security-s)). Needs the account made only when its emailed link is followed, and an email to an existing address offering sign-in or a reset instead. |
| Withdrawing an invitation sent by email | PROPOSED | A resident, host or organiser cannot take back an emailed invitation before it lapses or is replaced. Needs a withdraw action on the invitation row and its link refused neutrally afterwards. |
| Staff screens and staff messages with times in the staff language | PROPOSED | Customer pages and messages write times as each language does; the staff dashboard, urgent notices, coaches' emails and staff calendars still write them the English way ([known limitations](known-limitations.md#languages)). Needs the one formatter (`clockTime`) passed the staff language. |
| A turnstile rather than a door | DEFERRED | The only real answer to tailgating (one credential, one body); a hardware decision for each venue. |
| Reserve with Google's real-time Booking Server, and uploading the feeds | BLOCKED | Actions Center partner approval. The feeds exist as a draft ([external API](../integrations/external-api.md#reserve-with-google-readiness-only)). |
| Stripe Connect payouts to venues | DEFERRED | Venues take payment into their own accounts today ([ADR 0006](../architecture/decisions/0006-per-venue-payment-accounts.md)). |
| Singapore InvoiceNow (Peppol) e-invoicing | BLOCKED | An agreement with a Peppol access point provider; MyInvois covers Malaysia. |
| Web push notifications | PLANNED | No provider account: VAPID keys of the install's own, a service worker (the app has a manifest but none yet), a stored subscription per browser, and an opt-in on the confirmation screen. A migration and a new dependency or an encryption routine; a product decision on what is worth a push, since email and WhatsApp already carry every message. |
| Bowling sold by the game, and a lane given on arrival | PLANNED | Bowling is *specialised* in the [support matrix](../features/activities.md#support-matrix): lanes sell by time today. Needs a booking unit of games rather than minutes (a game count on the booking, priced per game, with an estimated time per game to hold the lane), and the desk assigning any free lane at check-in. |
| Day passes with no set time (climbing, bouldering), and gym entry on a membership | PLANNED | Today a session is a booked time with places, and a gym member books a session. Needs entry that takes a place while checked in rather than for a set interval: a capacity count fed by check-in and check-out alongside the claims, and a membership that admits without a booking. |
| Launch and tide windows for water hire | PLANNED | Today staff block hours or rain off. Needs a daily window (from a tide table or set by staff) that narrows the bookable hours of water activities. |
| Scheduling a range officer, lifeguard or instructor per session | PLANNED | Supervised activities are refused unstaffed hours, but nothing checks a named person is rostered. Needs staff rosters, and sessions bookable only where somebody qualified is on. |
| Track cycling heats, fencing bouts, route and grade records | DEFERRED | Sport-specific competition and logbook features; not needed to sell the activity, so they do not count against its support level. Build when a venue selling it asks. |
| Activity names checked by native speakers, and operators' own in every language | PLANNED | Built-in names in Malay, Chinese and Tamil are machine-assisted; an operator's own activity is named in English unless they give more. |
| Countries beyond Malaysia and Singapore (Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam) | PLANNED | The catalogue already names activities per country (*Takraw*, *Sipa*); each country also needs its row in the region table (currency, tax, phone numbers, zones), a gateway, and its languages ([regions](../product/regions-and-tax.md)). Indonesia's three zones are fine; a zone with daylight saving is refused until it is handled. |
| A country that changes its clocks | DEFERRED | The booking engine handles it: a booking touching a change of the clocks is refused and not offered ([booking time model](../architecture/booking-time-model.md#time)). What remains is the country itself (its row in the region table, tax, gateway and languages, as above); not until such a country is chosen. |
| Unstaffed hours to the minute, judged over the whole booking | PLANNED | Today whole hours, judged by the start ([known limitations](known-limitations.md)). Needs staffed times in minutes (a migration) and the door-code check over the booking's span. |
| Benchmarking under production-like conditions | PLANNED | The benchmark baseline ([results](../development/testing.md#results)) is one machine with the database beside the app. The next run needs real network latency to a managed database, a connection pool of production size, and two or more app instances behind a load balancer, before any further optimisation; nothing is to be optimised without that evidence. |
| Archiving past claims | PLANNED | `claims` cannot be partitioned by month ([ADR 0004](../architecture/decisions/0004-plain-postgres-not-timescaledb.md)); a retention job that moves inactive and past claims out, once a venue's data is large enough to need it. |
| The rest of the competition framework: more formats (consolation draws, double elimination, Swiss, ladders); counted performances (bouldering tops and zones); ratings and seeding from results; a level that is enforced | PLANNED | Built so far: the engine, sport templates, round robins, teams, performance events, progression issues, competitions with divisions, entry deadlines, age ranges, members only, guardian agreement for declared minors, points by set score, set and point ratios, a walkover's score, results by players (standing when unanswered for a day) and by officials, match lengths by stage and fixture, moving fixtures, a third-place match, and box leagues on the same scoring rules ([tournaments](../features/tournaments.md), [ADR 0013](../architecture/decisions/0013-competition-framework.md)). Each part here is a rule kind or a structure on that engine, not a new engine. **Evaluated and deferred (2026-10-24):** *double elimination* needs a losers' bracket fed from every winners' round and a grand final that may be played twice; with corrections, one changed result moves players in both brackets, so progression issues would have to follow two chains at once. *Swiss* pairs each round from the standings, so a correction to round 2 can invalidate round 3's pairings after they are played; it needs pairing rules (no rematches, colour or side balance), tie-breaks (Buchholz) and a rule for re-pairing. Neither is built until its whole progression and correction behaviour is specified and tested; an enforced level needs levels that are earned, not self-rated. |
| Tournament day: the next match announced, a court called when it is free, rankings across events | PROPOSED | Proposal, not scheduled. Check-in, moving fixtures and results by players and officials are built ([tournaments](../features/tournaments.md#on-the-day)); nothing announces the next match or carries from one event to the next. |
| Team leagues and fixture lists | PROPOSED | Proposal, not scheduled. Leagues are box leagues of singles or doubles. Needs teams as entrants and a fixture generator with home courts. |
| Matchmaking on earned levels | PROPOSED | Suggestions of games and of players by self-declared level, distance, schedule, intensity and price are built ([matchmaking](../features/matchmaking.md#suggested-games-and-players)). Still a proposal: levels earned from results (ratings the competition framework's results could feed) rather than self-rated, and proposing opponents or partners of a similar rating. |
| Messages between players | PROPOSED | Proposal, not scheduled. Invitations, private games and links, waiting lists with held places, weekly games, community groups, emails of suggested games, reminders the day before a game, and blocking and reporting are built ([matchmaking](../features/matchmaking.md#invitations-links-and-private-games)); players still have no way to write to each other in MySenam. Messages need moderation and reporting designed with them. |
| Teams that persist across tournaments | PROPOSED | Proposal, not scheduled. A team today is a name and a roster its captain types for one tournament, and a community group is not a tournament entrant. Needs teams whose members join with their own accounts, managed by a captain, that tournament and league entries can name. |
| Group news and sessions | PROPOSED | Proposal, not scheduled: a group's own page of news and its regular sessions. Today a group invites its members to games, and a weekly game repeats on a weekly booking. |
| Moving a class, a lesson's time moved by staff, a class's instructor as one of the venue's coaches, an attendance register | PROPOSED | Proposal, not scheduled. A class or lesson is cancelled and booked again today; a class instructor is a name. |
| Paying coaches | DEFERRED | The payouts report lists what each coach is owed; paying them is the venue's, outside MySenam, as for Stripe Connect payouts above. |
| LINE and Zalo messaging | BLOCKED | Each needs its own business account with the provider (LINE Official Account; Zalo OA), and both matter for a move into Thailand or Vietnam rather than Malaysia or Singapore, where customers are reached by WhatsApp and email. |
| Multi-visit passes sold online | PROPOSED | From the launch audit: memberships, credit and gift vouchers are sold online, passes only at the counter. Needs a `pass` payment purpose (a migration), checkout, and issuing the pass when paid ([pricing](../features/pricing.md#multi-visit-passes)) |
| Malaysian gateways beyond Billplz (iPay88, senangPay, toyyibPay, Curlec, eGHL, HitPay) | PROPOSED | From the launch audit: an adapter each behind the gateway layer (`src/lib/gateways`) and a live check each; for venues already contracted to one ([payment gateways](../integrations/payment-gateways.md)) |
| IoT court lighting | PROPOSED | From the launch audit: local competitors sell lights switched with a booking. Needs a hardware partner (a Tuya or relay controller), like smart locks above |
| Apple sign-in | PROPOSED | From the launch audit: the identity table already allows `apple`; needs an Apple developer account ([authentication](../architecture/authentication.md)) |
| A native app, offline mode | PROPOSED | From the launch audit: the site is responsive with a manifest and no service worker. Web push (above) comes first; a native app is a separate product |
| Live accounting integration | PROPOSED | From the launch audit: today an export for SQL Account, AutoCount, Xero and QuickBooks to import ([reporting](../features/reporting.md)). Xero or QuickBooks API clients; SQL Account and AutoCount have no common API |

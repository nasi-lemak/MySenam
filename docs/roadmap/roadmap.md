# Roadmap

Ideas not yet built, and a record of what has been delivered from them.
**Nothing in the ideas table is operational.** What works is in
[feature status](../product/feature-status.md); what is broken or unverified
is in [known limitations](known-limitations.md), and fixing those comes before
anything here.

| Idea | Status | Why, and what it needs |
| ---- | ------ | ---------------------- |
| Per-person credentials through a smart-lock vendor (TTLock, Igloohome; Igloohome's offline algoPIN suits a door with no wifi), so an unstaffed resource issues one credential per person instead of one code per lock | BLOCKED | Vendor credentials. The induction record and the customer's entry screen are already the right shape for it. It is what 24-hour gym chains use, and the biggest gap in unstaffed access. |
| A turnstile rather than a door | DEFERRED | The only real answer to tailgating (one credential, one body); a hardware decision for each venue. |
| Reserve with Google's real-time Booking Server, and uploading the feeds | BLOCKED | Actions Center partner approval. The feeds exist as a draft ([external API](../integrations/external-api.md#reserve-with-google-readiness-only)). |
| Stripe Connect payouts to venues | DEFERRED | Venues take payment into their own accounts today ([ADR 0006](../architecture/decisions/0006-per-venue-payment-accounts.md)). |
| Singapore InvoiceNow (Peppol) e-invoicing | BLOCKED | An agreement with a Peppol access point provider; MyInvois covers Malaysia. |
| Web push notifications | PLANNED | No provider account: VAPID keys of the install's own, a service worker (the app has a manifest but none yet), a stored subscription per browser, and an opt-in on the confirmation screen. A migration and a new dependency or an encryption routine; a product decision on what is worth a push, since email and WhatsApp already carry every message. |
| Bowling sold by the game, and a lane given on arrival | PLANNED | Bowling is *specialised* in the [support matrix](../features/activities.md#support-matrix): lanes sell by time today. Needs a booking unit of games rather than minutes (a game count on the booking, priced per game, with an estimated time per game to hold the lane), and the desk assigning any free lane at check-in. |
| Day passes with no set time (climbing, bouldering), and gym entry on a membership | PLANNED | Today a session is a booked time with places, and a gym member books a session. Needs entry that takes a place while checked in rather than for a set interval: a capacity count fed by check-in and check-out alongside the claims, and a membership that admits without a booking. |
| Kinds and sizes within one fleet (single and double kayaks, board sizes, bike frames) | PLANNED | Today each kind is a resource of its own, with its own price and count, which works but lists them separately. Needs variants on a hire resource, chosen at booking. |
| Launch and tide windows for water hire | PLANNED | Today staff block hours or rain off. Needs a daily window (from a tide table or set by staff) that narrows the bookable hours of water activities. |
| Scheduling a range officer, lifeguard or instructor per session | PLANNED | Supervised activities are refused unstaffed hours, but nothing checks a named person is rostered. Needs staff rosters, and sessions bookable only where somebody qualified is on. |
| Track cycling heats, fencing bouts, route and grade records | DEFERRED | Sport-specific competition and logbook features; not needed to sell the activity, so they do not count against its support level. Build when a venue selling it asks. |
| Activity names checked by native speakers, and operators' own in every language | PLANNED | Built-in names in Malay, Chinese and Tamil are machine-assisted; an operator's own activity is named in English unless they give more. |
| Countries beyond Malaysia and Singapore (Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam) | PLANNED | The catalogue already names activities per country (*Takraw*, *Sipa*); each country also needs its row in the region table (currency, tax, phone numbers, zones), a gateway, and its languages ([regions](../product/regions-and-tax.md)). Indonesia's three zones are fine; a zone with daylight saving is refused until it is handled. |
| Daylight-saving time zones | PLANNED | Needed only for a country that changes its clocks. Decide what a booking at a wall-clock time that is skipped or repeated means (refuse the skipped hour, take the first of a repeated one), test both changes, then let `keepsOneOffset` accept such zones. The clock functions already take any zone. |
| Unstaffed hours to the minute, judged over the whole booking | PLANNED | Today whole hours, judged by the start ([known limitations](known-limitations.md)). Needs staffed times in minutes (a migration) and the door-code check over the booking's span. |
| Archiving past claims | PLANNED | `claims` cannot be partitioned by month ([ADR 0004](../architecture/decisions/0004-plain-postgres-not-timescaledb.md)); a retention job that moves inactive and past claims out, once a venue's data is large enough to need it. |
| LINE and Zalo messaging | BLOCKED | Each needs its own business account with the provider (LINE Official Account; Zalo OA), and both matter for a move into Thailand or Vietnam rather than Malaysia or Singapore, where customers are reached by WhatsApp and email. |

## Delivered

Work taken from this page and from [known limitations](known-limitations.md),
in the order it was done, with what shows it works and what is still open.
Done means the acceptance criterion below was checked, not that code exists.

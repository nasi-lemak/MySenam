## 1. Courtsite: product, ownership, scale

### Takeaway
Courtsite is both a player marketplace (courtsite.my, now a web app/PWA with no native app) and a paid venue management SaaS ("Courtsite for Business", "Courtsite Console"). It was founded in August 2017 by Courtsite Web Solutions Sdn. Bhd. and claims 389 centres, 807k users and 12.35M booking hours. It is the clear incumbent and has government and federation anchor clients (PSM, BAM).

## 2. Courtsite: venue pricing, business model and payments

### Takeaway
Courtsite is **not free for venues**. It charges a monthly subscription (RM99 Starter / RM199 Advanced / custom Elite) plus a transaction fee whose amount is **not published** ("as agreed in writing"). Courtsite collects player payments on the venue's behalf, deducts its fees, and pays out within 3 business days after each settlement cycle. Players pay by FPX, Visa/Mastercard, Touch 'n Go, GrabPay, Boost or MCash. Bookings are non-refundable by default.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED pricing page (title "Courtsite Pricing - Plans Starting at RM99/Month"):
  - "Starter … RM99.00 / monthly + transaction fee": Scheduler Management, Data Analytics, Real-time Reporting
  - "Advanced … RM199.00 / monthly + transaction fee": everything in Starter plus "IoT Lighting Automation", "Multi-sports Scheduler"
  - "Elite … Custom / monthly": "Dedicated Customer Success Manager", "QR Code Check-in", "Priority Support", "Customized Features"
  - "Pay yearly (save 15%)"; the yearly prices are not shown
  - Platform rows: Partner Listing, Live Availability, "Multi-days Booking in a Single Checkout", "Online Payment (Credit/Debit Card, FPX & eWallets)", Social Games, Self-service Reschedule, Promo codes. Console rows: Analytics & Reports, Staff, Customers, Support. Per-plan ticks did not render in text.
  - Source: [business.courtsite.my/pricing](https://business.courtsite.my/pricing)
- Transaction fee amount: **UNKNOWN**, not on the pricing page. The terms define "Transaction Fees" as "the applicable fees in consideration of the Courtsite Platform Service and/or Courtsite Payment Processing Service as agreed in writing between you and us" — [Courtsite for Business Terms](https://business.courtsite.my/terms/service)
- Setup fee: UNKNOWN. Free tier: none shown on the pricing page.
- VERIFIED business terms ("last updated on 14 December 2022"):
  - Subscription is billed "monthly in advance", is "strictly non-refundable", and can change "upon 30 days' notice"
  - Transaction Fees are billed "at each Settlement Cycle interval"
  - Courtsite receives "the Purchase Amounts paid by your End-Customer in your name and for your account", may "set off any Service Fees against the Purchase Amounts", and "shall pay the Settlement Sum for a Settlement Cycle to you within three (3) Business Days from the end of that Settlement Cycle"
  - Listing on the Platform requires a Console subscription: "Suspension or termination of your subscription to the Courtsite Console Service shall result in the suspension and/or termination of your access to … Courtsite Platform Service and Courtsite Payment Processing Service"
  - Either party can end with 30 days' notice
  - Venue handles its own refunds: "you shall deal with your End-Customer directly on any modification, postponement, cancellation or refund"
  - Source: [business.courtsite.my/terms/service](https://business.courtsite.my/terms/service)
- Settlement cycle length (daily, weekly, etc.): **UNKNOWN**
- VERIFIED player payment methods: "FPX Online Banking", "Credit / Debit Cards", "Touch 'n Go eWallet", "GrabPay", "Boost", "M cash". "Courtsite currently supports MasterCard and Visa only". Players have "8 minutes to complete the payment after clicking Checkout Cart" — [Courtsite Help: Payment](https://www.courtsite.my/support/faq/payment). DuitNow QR and ShopeePay are not listed.
- VERIFIED gateway: Courtsite partnered with Curlec (blog dated 8 May 2024): "Through Curlec's direct integration with Paynet, venue owners can now enjoy faster payments", with "real-time payment processing" — [Courtsite blog](https://blog.courtsite.my/courtsite-and-curlec-unite-to-provide-uncompromised-payments/). Fees were not stated.
- VERIFIED player terms ("Updated August 2024"): "Booking Fees" means "the fees that is paid by the User to the Partner Centre through the Platform". "All Bookings placed on the Platform are treated as confirmed and final … Booking Fees are non-refundable, except in circumstances where there is an occurrence of a technical glitch at no fault of the User" — [courtsite.my/terms/service](https://www.courtsite.my/terms/service)
- VERIFIED help centre: "Sorry, we currently do not allow for any refunds or cancellations of bookings". Postponement is possible "with at least two days advance notice or subject to the center's policy" — [Courtsite Help: Booking](https://www.courtsite.my/support/faq/booking)
- VERIFIED: SST is charged on online bookings. The help centre explains that bookings made 1–6 Jan 2026 were charged 8% instead of 6% and describes an "SST Refund Program" paid as same-centre vouchers — [Courtsite Help](https://www.courtsite.my/support/faq/booking)
- Player-side convenience or booking fee: **UNKNOWN**. No convenience, service or platform fee is mentioned in the player terms or the payment FAQ ([terms](https://www.courtsite.my/terms/service), [payment FAQ](https://www.courtsite.my/support/faq/payment)). Checkout could not be tested without an account.

### Inferences
- The model is a hybrid: SaaS subscription, plus a per-transaction take that is not disclosed, plus custody of player funds (Courtsite is merchant-of-collection and remits net). A new entrant can contrast its own published per-booking cost against Courtsite's "agreed in writing" fee. That is an honest transparency point, but the actual size of Courtsite's fee is UNKNOWN and must not be stated as a number.
- Listing on the marketplace is tied to the paid Console subscription, so there is no free "list only" tier.

### Gaps
- Exact transaction fee (percentage or flat), settlement cycle length, setup or onboarding fees, IoT hardware cost, and yearly prices: all UNKNOWN.
- Whether players pay any surcharge at checkout: UNKNOWN.

## 3. Courtsite: features, messaging, sports, geography, sentiment

Courtsite is feature-rich: live availability, multi-day checkout, self-service reschedule and extend, social games, events, leagues (Courtsite Teams), promo codes and vouchers, IoT lighting, QR check-in, staff and analytics, and LHDN e-invoice. Confirmations go by email. Customer support runs on WhatsApp and live chat. No player WhatsApp or SMS notifications were documented. It is web/PWA only.

## 5. Courtease (courtease.my)

### Takeaway
Courtease is a very small, recent marketplace plus venue SaaS run by "COURTEASE ENTERPRISE" in Kampar, Perak. Its pitch is flat RM99/month per venue with "RM 0 commission", a 14-day free trial, and a Perak early-adopter discount. Payments go through ToyyibPay FPX and settle to the venue's bank in 1–3 business days. It is the closest in positioning to a "no-commission, flat-fee" new entrant.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: "© 2026 COURTEASE ENTERPRISE Kampar, Perak, Malaysia"; meta description "Book badminton, futsal, basketball, and tennis courts across Malaysia. Instant confirmation, secure payment" — [courtease.my](https://courtease.my/). Note: courtease.com is a parked or unrelated domain that redirects to "/lander" ([courtease.com](https://courtease.com)).
- VERIFIED About: "full-service — receipts, payment processing, customer management, and tax-compliant invoicing — without platform commissions"; "Headquartered in Kampar, Perak" — [courtease.my/about](https://courtease.my/about)
- VERIFIED For Business page ([courtease.my/business](https://courtease.my/business)):

## 6. Other Malaysian court and venue booking platforms

### Takeaway
The rest of the market is fragmented: venue-SaaS vendors (Vyrox SPORTSERVA, Book & Go white-label), operator-run booking (Footballhub/Metahub), foreign platforms used by specific venues (PlayByPoint), club and open-play tools (Reclub), and an aggregator (OpenCourt365) that names the "major" platforms as Courtsite, AFA Sports, PlayByPoint, Metahub and SportsWePlay.

### Cited Findings
- **OpenCourt365** (aggregator): "Compare prices across Courtsite, AFA Sports, PlayByPoint, Metahub & SportsWePlay"; "Click through to book on the original platform — no middleman, no extra fees". Starts with pickleball. Mentions KL, Selangor, Penang and Johor Bahru — [opencourt365.com.my](https://opencourt365.com.my/) (VERIFIED, own site)
- **Vyrox SPORTSERVA** (VYROX International Sdn Bhd, Bangsar South, KL): "a complete booking and management platform for sports facilities". Testimonials are unfilled placeholders and no clients are named.

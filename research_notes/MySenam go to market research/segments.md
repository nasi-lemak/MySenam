# MySenam customer segments: Malaysia (and brief SEA notes)

Retrieval date for all URLs: 2026-10-18.

How the labels are used:
- **VERIFIED**: I fetched and read the page myself, and the page states the fact directly.
- **REPORTED**: the fact comes from a search-result summary or a secondary source I did not fully read, or from a party with an interest in the claim (a vendor or the operator itself). The figure is cited as given and was not cross-checked.
- **HYPOTHESIS**: my own inference. No source states it.
- **UNKNOWN**: I searched and found no reliable figure.

Scope note: I made about 20 search and fetch calls. Coverage is good for pickleball, condos, councils and gyms (counts and chains). It is thin for badminton and futsal venue counts and for chain booking systems. No interviews were done, and no quotes below are invented.

## 1. Market size indicators by segment

### Takeaway
Pickleball is the only segment with documented, very fast venue growth: Kuala Lumpur reportedly went from 6 venues in June 2024 to over 200 by August 2025. Pickleball also has the highest court rates (RM30–80/hour). Strata (about 26,000 schemes) and gyms (about 4,900) are large, but other vendors already serve them. I found no reliable national count of badminton or futsal venues, which is a material gap because these are the core of the market.

So badminton is not #1 for participation in this survey; football and futsal lead. — [UM eprints: Sport, exercise, recreation and e-sport participation in Malaysia](https://eprints.um.edu.my/44967/); [HRMARS paper](https://hrmars.com/papers_submitted/12288/the-sports-participation-trend-among-malaysians-by-gender-ethnic-and-age-group.pdf)

## 2. How each segment books today, and the tools they use

### Takeaway
Pickleball arenas in the Klang Valley mostly already use an online booking platform. In a 26-venue sample, Courtsite powers about half, then AFA/PlaySportsTogether, Playbypoint and a few others. Condos use bundled community apps priced at about RM500–900 per month per site. Councils use their own portals. Badminton and futsal booking habits (WhatsApp, phone, walk-in) are widely assumed but poorly documented online.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Vulcan Post (2025-03-18, a sponsored piece) lists 26 bookable Klang Valley pickleball venues. Booking platforms: Courtsite 13, AFA/PlaySportsTogether 7, Playbypoint 2, and one each of an own app (Pickle Park), Bookeo, Appolous and an own website (High On Pickle). The article does not mention WhatsApp or Reclub booking. Court counts range from 2 to 27 per venue. — [Vulcan Post, 2025-03-18](https://vulcanpost.com/883329/pickleball-courts-online-booking-price-rental-malaysia-klang-valley-2025/)
- REPORTED: Reclub is a social and session-organizing platform (clubs, meetups, open play), not primarily a court-booking system. Its Singapore pickleball community has over 12,000 members. — [Reclub Singapore](https://reclub.co/communities/sg/singapore)
- VERIFIED: Courtsite for Business gives venues an admin panel, a scheduler for online and offline bookings, a revenue dashboard, a player database, and marketplace listing and marketing exposure. The FAQ page shows no public pricing or commission terms, and no POS is mentioned. — [Courtsite for Business FAQ](https://business.courtsite.my/support/faq)
- VERIFIED: i-Neighbour (condo community app) lists plans at RM580 / 750 / 900 per month (limited-time offers RM500 / 650 / 800) for Klang Valley communities of 800 units or fewer. "Book facilities within the community" appears as a resident feature. — [i-Neighbour pricing](https://www.i-neighbour.com/pricing)
- REPORTED: JaGaApp has a facility-booking module (QR/JaGaID). It was reported in about 2021 to be in 400+ neighbourhoods with 150,000+ users. i-Neighbour was reported at a 200-strong Malaysian customer base around the same time. Both figures are several years old. — [Vulcan Post on JaGaApp](https://vulcanpost.com/704196/jagaapp-mobile-app-neighbourhood-security-community/); [Yahoo Malaysia](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/smart-community-does-improve-safety-223205081.html); [JaGaApp 2.0, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/jagaapp-2-0/id1518198706)
- REPORTED: Condo Master (Malaysian strata management software) price sheet, July 2025: base RM500 per month for buildings up to 300 units, plus 8% SST. — [Condo Master PricingSheet Jul2025](https://condo-master.com/Content/PricingSheet_Jul2025.pdf)
- REPORTED: StoreHub publishes a blog page on "top POS systems for gyms and fitness centres Malaysia", which suggests local POS vendors target gyms. — [StoreHub blog](https://www.storehub.com/my/blog/top-pos-system-for-gyms-fitness-centres-malaysia)
- REPORTED: Councils (DBKL, MBPJ): see section 1. Both have online portals, and MBPJ still also books by phone.
- UNKNOWN: Kiple, Timetec, Propertyware and Mobile Ezy facility-booking features and pricing (searched; nothing citable returned). Glofox and Mindbody adoption or pricing in Malaysia (not searched in time). Booking systems of gym chains (likely in-house or franchisor systems; unverified).
- UNKNOWN: The share of badminton and futsal halls that book by WhatsApp, phone, Google Forms or spreadsheets versus software. I found no survey. Sample listings (e.g. Sport 100 Klang: "no refund or cancellation after booking confirmed") show that some badminton halls take online bookings via Courtsite. — [Sport 100 on Courtsite](https://www.courtsite.my/centre/Sport%20100/01DTRKZTTE7X9S5QFD392ANGY0/select)

### Inferences
- HYPOTHESIS: Pickleball is not a greenfield "WhatsApp-only" segment in the Klang Valley. Most visible arenas already use Courtsite or AFA. MySenam would be a switcher pitch there, or it should target new venues before they pick a platform, and pickleball outside the Klang Valley (Johor, Penang, Perak), where coverage is likely thinner.
- HYPOTHESIS: Older independent badminton halls, especially Chinese-owned halls run by associations or families, are the most likely to still run on WhatsApp, phone and a paper or whiteboard schedule with fixed weekly regulars. This needs validation by calling 20–30 halls.
- HYPOTHESIS: Venues that are on Courtsite mainly for discovery may keep that listing and still adopt a separate management tool, if MySenam can sync or at least coexist. They are not necessarily lost prospects.

### Gaps
- No primary data on WhatsApp/phone/manual booking prevalence among badminton and futsal halls.
- The booking systems of futsal chains (Sports Planet, Samba De Futsal) were not checked on their websites.
- Gym software landscape (Glofox, Mindbody, local options) in Malaysia was not researched.

## 4. Willingness-to-pay signals

### Takeaway
Software spend benchmarks in Malaysia: StoreHub POS about RM102–108 per month, Loyverse free core plus about USD5 add-ons, condo apps RM500–900 per month per site. Court revenue bounds what is plausible. Pickleball courts earn RM30–80/hour, futsal RM70–106/hour, and badminton roughly RM15–30/hour (Johor data). A 6-court pickleball venue can plausibly afford RM100–300 per month; a small badminton hall is far more price-sensitive.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED: Klang Valley pickleball rates (March 2025, 26 venues): off-peak mostly RM29–55/hour, peak RM49–80/hour. Lowest: PickleTime "skinny" courts at RM20 all day. Highest peak: RM80 (Grand Rally, KL City Pickleball covered, Pickle Park centre court). — [Vulcan Post, 2025-03-18](https://vulcanpost.com/883329/pickleball-courts-online-booking-price-rental-malaysia-klang-valley-2025/)
- REPORTED: Futsal: Sports Planet RM70 (weekday) to RM100 (weekend) per hour. SportsCity Futsal PJ RM106 (Monday–Saturday) and RM85 (Sunday). Samba De Futsal from RM80. The listing is undated. — [SAYS](https://says.com/my/sports/futsal-courts-in-klang-valley)
- REPORTED: Badminton, Johor Bahru (Toppen area, undated): weekday off-peak RM15/hour, peak RM30/hour, late night RM20/hour, weekends RM26–28/hour. I found no 2025 Klang Valley badminton rate card. — [TheSmartLocal, Toppen JB](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/forum-tebrau-toppen-shopping-centre-jb/)
- REPORTED (SEA comparison): Singapore Smash Arena badminton S$16 off-peak and S$32 peak per hour. — [Dollars and Sense SG](https://dollarsandsense.sg/price-guide-booking-badminton-courts-singapore/)
- REPORTED: StoreHub POS from about RM102–108 per month billed annually (RM1,224/year), hardware from about RM1,450–1,690. Loyverse core is free, with add-ons of about USD5 per employee per month, advanced inventory about RM110 per store, integrations about RM40 per store. These figures come from StoreHub's own competitor comparison pages. — [StoreHub vs Loyverse](https://www.storehub.com/my/blog/storehub-vs-loyverse)
- VERIFIED: i-Neighbour condo app RM500–900 per month per community (800 units or fewer, Klang Valley). — [i-Neighbour pricing](https://www.i-neighbour.com/pricing)
- REPORTED: Condo Master RM500 per month base (300 units or fewer) plus SST. — [Condo Master PDF](https://condo-master.com/Content/PricingSheet_Jul2025.pdf)
- REPORTED: Council hall rates are published (MBPJ 2024 PDF), so council halls are low-price public facilities. — [MBPJ rate PDF](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/sites/default/files/kadar_tempahan_kemudahan_mbpj_tahun_2024.pdf)
- UNKNOWN: Courtsite's commission or subscription to venues (not on its public FAQ). Utilisation rates for Malaysian courts.

### Inferences
- HYPOTHESIS (illustrative arithmetic, not data): a 6-court pickleball venue at an average of RM50/hour and 40% utilisation over 16 open hours makes about RM1,900 per day, or roughly RM57k per month. A 10-court badminton hall at RM20/hour on the same assumptions makes about RM38k per month. Software at RM150–300 per month is about 0.3–0.8% of revenue, and venues tolerate that when they see more bookings or fewer no-shows. Booking commissions (if a marketplace charges a few % per booking) would cost more at scale, so a "flat fee, no commission" pitch has a real economic basis for busy venues.
- HYPOTHESIS: The reference price anchors for an independent venue owner are POS spend (about RM100 per month) and the free WhatsApp status quo. Pricing above about RM200 per month for single small halls will likely meet resistance. Pickleball arenas and chains can bear more.
- HYPOTHESIS: Strata buyers already pay RM500–900 per month for bundled apps, so willingness to pay exists. But the decision sits with JMB/MC committees and managing agents (594 firms), not with a single owner.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Klang Valley badminton rates, so check 10 Courtsite listings.
- No utilisation data.
- Competitor (Courtsite, AFA, Playbypoint) venue-side pricing is not public in what I found.

- HYPOTHESIS: For badminton halls, the channels are likely coaches and academies running classes at the hall, plus Chinese-language WhatsApp and Facebook communities. Validate with interviews.
- HYPOTHESIS: Strata is reached through managing agents (594 licensed firms covering 26k schemes). One agent relationship could unlock dozens of schemes, but those agents already bundle apps.

### Gaps

## 6. Switching costs and onboarding obstacles

### Takeaway
The clearest documented obstacles are: incumbent marketplace listings (Courtsite has around half of visible Klang Valley pickleball venues), councils' own portals, and condo apps bundled with security and billing. E-invoicing pressure on small venues fell sharply in September 2026, when the exemption threshold rose to RM3 million, so it is not a forcing function for most independent halls. Barriers such as WhatsApp habit, cash, fixed regular slots, language and older owners are plausible but undocumented in sources I found.

### Cited Findings
- REPORTED: From 1 September 2026, MSMEs with annual revenue below RM3 million are exempt from e-invoicing, up from RM1 million. LHDN estimates more than 1.1 million businesses benefit, and participation is voluntary below the threshold. — [The Star, 2026-08-30](https://thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/30/over-11-million-businesses-to-benefit-from-higher-e-invoicing-threshold-says-lhdn); [Malay Mail, 2026-08-31](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/08/31/irb-stresses-voluntary-participation-as-government-lifts-einvoice-threshold-easing-compliance-for-msmes/233340)
- VERIFIED: 13 of 26 sampled Klang Valley pickleball venues book via Courtsite and 7 via AFA. — [Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/883329/pickleball-courts-online-booking-price-rental-malaysia-klang-valley-2025/)
- REPORTED: Some venues impose strict no-refund or no-reschedule rules (Sport 100: no refund or cancellation once confirmed), which suggests no-show and cancellation pain that software could address. — [Sport 100 on Courtsite](https://www.courtsite.my/centre/Sport%20100/01DTRKZTTE7X9S5QFD392ANGY0/select)
- REPORTED: Pickleball on converted badminton courts creates conflicts (surface damage, noise), and some operators refuse it. Multi-sport scheduling is therefore a real need. — [FMT, 2025-04-19](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/leisure/2025/04/19/what-a-pickle-not-everyones-thrilled-with-the-rapid-rise-of-pickleball)
- UNKNOWN: Prevalence of cash payment, counter hardware, staff turnover, and language preference (Malay, Chinese, English) among venue operators. No sources found.

### Inferences
- HYPOTHESIS: The biggest switching cost is migrating fixed weekly regulars (recurring bookings) and the venue's customer phone list. An import or "recurring slot" onboarding flow is likely decisive.
- HYPOTHESIS: Venues may treat marketplaces as a demand channel and resist leaving. A positioning of "keep your Courtsite listing, run your venue on MySenam" (or a calendar sync) reduces perceived risk.
- HYPOTHESIS: Chinese-language support (UI and WhatsApp templates) likely matters for older badminton-hall owners. Malay matters for council and community halls. This is unvalidated.
- HYPOTHESIS: Do not count on e-invoicing as an urgency driver for small independents in the first 12 months, since most fall under RM3 million revenue. It may matter for chains and larger arenas.

### Gaps
- No primary interviews or surveys on operator pain points, payment mix or language needs.

## 7. Brief SEA expansion notes

### Takeaway
Each SEA market has local booking incumbents. Singapore has the government's ActiveSG plus Reclub for social play. Indonesia has AYO, strong in padel and mini soccer. The Philippines has a crowded set of pickleball booking apps. Thailand has mostly white-label venue apps. Payment rails differ by country (PayNow, QRIS, GCash, PromptPay), which affects any later expansion.

### Cited Findings
- REPORTED: Singapore: Reclub hosts many pickleball clubs (12,000+ members in its Singapore pickleball community). It is a social and session platform rather than a venue booking system. — [Reclub Singapore](https://reclub.co/communities/sg/singapore); [Reclub App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/reclub-social-sports-nearby/id1323924315)
- REPORTED: Indonesia: AYO Indonesia (ayo.co.id / ayo.co) is a consumer venue-booking marketplace covering football and mini soccer, padel, badminton and tennis, with in-app payment. — [AYO venues](https://ayo.co/venues); [AYO padel](https://ayo.co/venues/sport/padel); [AYO booking guide](https://ayo.co/blog/cara-booking-lapangan-olahraga-secara-online-tanpa-ribet-di-ayo)
- REPORTED: Philippines: many pickleball booking platforms, including Pickle Time ("#1 pickleball booking platform"), PickleCourt, PicklePlay, Dinky, DINK PH, PickleHub, SociaPlay, and PlayServe (venue management). — [Pickle Time](https://www.pickletime.ph/); [PickleCourt](https://picklecourt.ph/open-play); [PlayServe](https://playserve.co/); [Dinky](https://www.dinky.ph/courts); [PickleHub](https://picklehub.ph/)
- REPORTED: Thailand: no dominant consumer court-booking app surfaced. White-label vendors (Book & Go) build branded apps for venues such as Kross Padel in Bangkok. — [Book & Go](https://www.bookandgo.app/en/booking/basketball-booking-system-bangkok); [Kross Padel app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6741785490)
- UNKNOWN: ActiveSG facility booking details, and the payment-rail integration costs of PayNow, QRIS, PromptPay and GCash. Not researched in this pass; this is general background knowledge, not sourced here.

### Inferences
- HYPOTHESIS: The Philippines' pickleball booking market looks fragmented and crowded, so it is a hard market to enter. Thailand's lack of a consumer marketplace may favour a SaaS-first, white-label-light product like MySenam. Singapore's public courts sit inside ActiveSG, which limits SaaS to private venues.

### Gaps

## 8. Segment attractiveness ranking for the first 12 months (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Ranked on evidence plus labelled hypotheses:
1. Independent pickleball venues, especially new ones and those outside the Klang Valley.
2. Independent badminton halls, plus multi-sport halls adding pickleball.
3. Small sports-venue chains of 2–10 sites.
4. Futsal centres.
5. Condominiums and strata.
6. Gyms and fitness studios.
7. Councils and community halls.

Confidence is medium for the top two and low for the badminton-versus-pickleball order, because badminton counts and booking habits are UNKNOWN.

### Cited Findings
- Pickleball: fastest venue formation (KL 6 to 200+ venues, June 2024 to August 2025) and highest rates (RM30–80/hour). But about 77% of sampled Klang Valley venues already use Courtsite or AFA. — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2025/08/09/pickleball-craze-takes-over-malaysian-courts); [Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/883329/pickleball-courts-online-booking-price-rental-malaysia-klang-valley-2025/)
- Badminton: halls of 8–28 courts exist, rates are low (about RM15–30/hour), and some are on Courtsite. — [Courtsite listings, section 1](https://www.courtsite.my/search); [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/forum-tebrau-toppen-shopping-centre-jb/)
- Futsal: high per-hour rates (RM70–106) and a known chain (Sports Planet, 10+ outlets). Participation is second only to football (11.0%). — [SAYS](https://says.com/my/sports/futsal-courts-in-klang-valley); [UM eprints](https://eprints.um.edu.my/44967/)
- Strata: 26,334 schemes, served by bundled apps at RM500–900 per month. — [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2025/04/08/housing-ministry-new-law-in-the-works-to-fix-poor-property-building-management-in-malaysia/172326); [i-Neighbour](https://www.i-neighbour.com/pricing)
- Councils: own portals (DBKL Tempah@KL and MyTempahan, MBPJ MyPJTempahan). — [DBKL](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/en/fasiliti-awam/dewan-serbaguna); [MBPJ](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/ms/rakyat/perkhidmatan-online-mbpj)
- Gyms: about 4,900 gyms. Chains are on franchisor systems (Anytime Fitness 100 clubs). — [Ezypay](https://www.ezypay.com/blog/malaysia-a-connected-wellness-driven-market-in-southeast-asia); [NST](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2026/01/1359844/anytime-fitness-continues-expansion-malaysia)

### Inferences
- HYPOTHESIS, ranking rationale:
  1. **Pickleball (new or non-Klang Valley venues):** high revenue per court, owners who are digital-native, recent fit-outs with no entrenched system, and an active community (Reclub, DUPR) that wants open play, ladders and tournaments. Risk: Courtsite's head start in the Klang Valley, and a possible fad plateau.
  2. **Badminton halls:** the largest installed base of court venues and likely the most manual operations (UNKNOWN). They are price-sensitive. Upside comes when they add pickleball and need multi-sport scheduling.
  3. **Small chains (2–10 sites):** these fit MySenam's branch, shared-credit and staff-scoping features and are worth more per account. But the pool is small, and some already use Courtsite.
  4. **Futsal:** high rates and team bookings with weekly regulars. Participation looks flat to declining (my assumption, UNVERIFIED). Few public signals.
  5. **Strata:** large count, but incumbent bundled apps and committee decisions mean long sales cycles. Possibly a later partner play with managing agents.
  6. **Gyms:** different workflow (memberships and classes), and a crowded vertical-SaaS field (Glofox and Mindbody not researched).
  7. **Councils:** in-house portals and tender procurement. Not viable in 12 months.
- HYPOTHESIS: The validation priority is to phone or visit 20–30 badminton halls and 10–15 new pickleball venues. The goals: measure WhatsApp/manual prevalence, Courtsite dependence, recurring-regulars share, language preferences and acceptable monthly price.

### Gaps
- The ranking leans on hypotheses because no primary operator data exists. Badminton and futsal venue counts and booking-method shares are the biggest unknowns that could change the order.
